# TCP vs UDP

easy way to explain taking example irl in my opition

---

Capture this, ya want to send a package to a fwiend. 

When using TCP, your careful. You warps the package nicely, label it with their address, and even get a receipt to confirm they got it. If the package gets lost, you resend it until they confirm they got it safe and sound. Its **PERFECT** n **SAFE** delivery but **SLOWER**.

When using UDP, you just throw the package in a bag and toss it over the fence. No wrapping, no address, no receipt. If it gets lost, oh well, u just hope it makes it. Its **FAST** but **RISKY**.

TCP is for when every data byte matters, eg: emails, files, web pages. 

UDP is for when speed is key and some data loss is okay, eg: video calls, gaming, streaming.

***

BOTH have their uses, depends on what u need: safety or speed!
